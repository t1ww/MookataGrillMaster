# MookataGrillMaster

> A Thai boat grill tycoon game focused on depth, freedom, and hands-on management.

---

## Overview

MookataGrillMaster is not a heavily guided experience filled with tutorials.  
Players are expected to observe, experiment, and learn naturally through gameplay.

Controls and available actions will always be visible when applicable, but the game will not constantly explain every mechanic.

---

## Starting Small

You begin with almost nothing:

- 1 table
- A stove
- A boat grill pan
- A small storage area

Your first tasks are simple:

- Prepare the stove
- Slice pork for customers
- Serve ingredients
- Collect payments

---

## Growing Your Restaurant

As your business grows, you'll gradually take on more responsibilities:

- Manage inventory and stock
- Purchase supplies yourself
- Expand your restaurant
- Handle larger customer demands
- Improve workflow and efficiency

From a humble roadside setup to a busy mookata restaurant, every part of the business is in your hands.

# This game uses Rojo for development
Generated by [Rojo](https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rojo) 7.7.0-rc.1.

## Getting Started
To build the place from scratch, use:

```bash
rojo build -o "MookataGrillMaster.rbxlx"
```

Next, open `MookataGrillMaster.rbxlx` in Roblox Studio and start the Rojo server:

```bash
rojo serve
```

For more help, check out [the Rojo documentation](https://rojo.space/docs).
